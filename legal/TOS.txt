MuxCore acceptable-use agreement
================================

MuxCore is a personal media library. It helps you organize and play files
you already have.

By installing and using MuxCore you agree that:

  1. You will only add media you own, or media you have a lawful right to
     keep and play (for example, rips of discs you purchased, or files a
     license explicitly allows you to store).

  2. You will not use MuxCore to obtain, store, share, or manage
     copyrighted material without authorization.

  3. MuxCore does not download movies, shows, music, or other media from
     the internet, BitTorrent, or Usenet. It does not search pirate
     indexers. It does not fetch files for you.

  4. You are solely responsible for the folders you point MuxCore at and
     for complying with copyright and other laws where you live.

If you do not agree, decline and the installer will exit without installing
MuxCore.
